The short answer

One stretch of coast, three names

From Carvoeiro east to Albandeira the cliffs are cut into caves, arches and coves. The Carvoeiro caves and Algar Seco are at the western end, Benagil Cave in the middle, Praia da Marinha about a kilometre east of it. A boat runs the whole line from the water; the Seven Hanging Valleys trail follows it along the clifftop between Praia da Marinha and Praia de Vale Centeanes.

Praia da Marinha

Praia da Marinha

Turismo do Algarve's walking guide uses Praia da Marinha as its example of the karst coast here, where fresh and salt water have worked the limestone into “arches, coves and islets”. Unlike the sand inside Benagil Cave, which nobody may set foot on, it is an ordinary beach you reach by road and on foot, and the start of the Seven Hanging Valleys trail. From the water you see it on most Benagil boats and on the longer kayak routes, which paddle on past the cave to Marinha.

Boat trips do not land you on these beaches in 2026. In January 2026 the Capitania do Porto de Portimão stopped licensing boat-tour landings on the beaches with no footpath in Lagoa and Albufeira (the “barbecue” trips) for the 2026 season, after its survey found the sand shrinking and largely inside the cliff-fall zones. The beaches it names include Praia do Pau, Praia do Barranco das Belharucas, Praia do Pontal and Praia do Barranco in Lagoa.

On foot

The Seven Hanging Valleys trail

The trail runs along the clifftop between Praia da Marinha and Praia de Vale Centeanes, passing Benagil and Praia do Carvalho. Turismo do Algarve's guide gives it as 6 km one way (12 km there and back), about three hours each way, with a 360 m cumulative climb from Marinha, on somewhat rocky dirt paths, signposted with information panels, and walkable all year except on very rainy or windy days. The Lagoa council is responsible for it. The guide's warning is the one to keep: the cliffs are unstable, so keep away from the edge.

On the way it notes Praia de Benagil, where the fishing boats now mostly take visitors to the caves, and Praia do Carvalho, reached through a tunnel cut in the soft limestone. From the top you look down through Benagil's skylight; to see the inside you need a boat or a guided kayak.

Carvoeiro & Algar Seco

The Carvoeiro caves and Algar Seco

Turismo do Algarve's touring guide describes Carvoeiro as “the place to start a fascinating boat trip through the 18 caves in these cliffs”, and names the Gruta do Pintal and the Gruta dos Roazes among them. About 800 m from Carvoeiro's beach, Algar Seco is the formation on land, rock “sculpted by the wind and the sea into fantastic shapes”, with the Varanda dos Namorados, the Lovers' Terrace. The boats leave straight off Praia do Carvoeiro; the Carvoeiro page has its operators.
