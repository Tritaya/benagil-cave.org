The short answer

Two and a half hours, the cave coast and a dolphin search

The combined trips run about 2.5 hours. They leave a marina, spend part of the time in open water looking for dolphins and the rest running the cliffs and caves between Albufeira and Benagil, often with a swim stop when the sea allows. The cave part is short: the rules give every motorised boat two minutes inside, and some of the bigger boats look in from the entrance.

The most-reviewed of them leaves Albufeira: Dream Wave Algarve's 2.5-hour trip on a 47-seat jet-powered boat, with marine biologists on board, from $38 on 11 November 2026. Its calendar is just below.

The dolphins

What the listings say about dolphins, and about refunds

Wild dolphins cannot be promised, and three of the listings on this page say so in different ways. The details matter if you are booking for a child who has their heart set on it:

  • Dream Wave Algarve (Albufeira): “Dolphins are wild animals, so there is no guarantee you'll see them”, and “There will be no refunds if there is no dolphin sighting”.
  • XRide Algarve (Albufeira): it “does not guarantee their observation”, and it can cancel and refund for adverse sea or weather.
  • Seatrips Lagos: “Dolphin watching is a search for wild animals in their natural environment”; it writes that its team knows the main feeding areas off Lagos.

The cancellation terms on these three listings (free up to 24 hours before) cover your change of plans and the sea, not the dolphins.

The cave

What happens at Benagil on a dolphin trip

The same rules as every other boat: two minutes inside through the western entrance, up to five boats at once, nobody on the sand and nobody swimming in. Each card says whether the boat enters the cave when the sea allows or, like the catamarans, views it from the entrance. When the sea closes the cave, the listings handle it differently. Dream Wave Algarve's says that if the Marine Police close the caves, “the caves will be viewed from a safe distance”. XRide Algarve's says that, as the caves are regulated by the port authorities, “if the caves are closed there will be no refund”. The full rules are on the rules page, and today's sea is at the top of the home page.
