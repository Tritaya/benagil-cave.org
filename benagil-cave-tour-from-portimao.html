From Portimão

Why leave from Portimão

Portimão has the biggest marina on this stretch of coast, and with it the widest choice of large, comfortable boats — catamarans with shade, toilets and a bar, plus speedboats and combo boat-and-kayak trips. The crossing is longer (you head out of the Arade river mouth, past Ferragudo, then east along the cliffs), but the boats are built for it.

It’s the natural choice if you’re staying in Portimão or Praia da Rocha, travelling with kids or older family, or simply prefer a roomy deck to a small open boat.

Logistics

Getting there & parking

The essentials

  • Departure point: Marina de Portimão (some kayak combos stage from Benagil beach). Boats also leave the Arade river mouth past Ferragudo.
  • Time to the cave: ~30–45 min each way by catamaran or boat.
  • Driving here: From Faro airport ~65 km / ~40–45 minutes via the A22. From Lagos ~20 min, from Albufeira ~30 min.
  • Parking: Marina de Portimão has on-site outdoor and covered parking (paid in season) — far easier than Benagil village.

Ferragudo note: tours marketed “from Ferragudo” generally depart the Portimão / Arade area — there’s no separate Ferragudo fleet. Check the actual embarkation point when you book.

Before You Book

The access rules apply here too

Wherever you leave from, the same binding rules govern the cave itself. Under Edital 019/2024 of the Capitania do Porto de Portimão, in force since August 2024 and amended by Edital 009/2025 of 2 July 2025: no landing on the beach inside, no swimming or floating in, no paddle-craft outing or rental without a guide. Motorised boats enter by the western entrance for two minutes, up to five at once. Kayaks and SUPs enter by the eastern entrance in groups of six with one first-aid-certified guide, up to fourteen craft inside at once, for up to eight minutes. Life jackets are compulsory for the whole visit. It is the same cave under the same law; the town only changes how you get there.

The full text, in Portuguese and English, with dates and sources, is on the rules page. If any Portimão operator promises a stop on the beach inside Benagil, that is the pre-2024 experience: check before paying.
