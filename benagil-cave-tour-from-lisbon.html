From Lisbon

A long day for a few hours on the coast

Lisbon to the Algarve is a classic long day out. The trips leave early, drive south through the Alentejo, and string Benagil together with Lagos, Ponta da Piedade, Carvoeiro and Algar Seco before the drive back. Benagil is one stop of several.

Is it worth it? If Lisbon is your only base and the Algarve coast is on your list, it is a fair one-day sample. If you will be in the Algarve later in the trip, wait: from Carvoeiro or Armação de Pêra the cave is 15 to 25 minutes by boat.

Logistics

Getting there & parking

The essentials

  • Pickup: Central Lisbon: a hotel pickup or a fixed meeting point, depending on the option you book. The most-reviewed trip meets small groups outside the Hard Rock Cafe on Avenida da Liberdade.
  • Time to the cave: About 2 h 50 min each way by road (roughly 266 km); the tours allow around three hours in the van each way.
  • By train instead: No train goes near Benagil. Intercidades and Alfa Pendular trains reach Tunes junction in about two and a half hours, and from there it is a taxi to the coast. Going it alone only makes sense with a night in the Algarve.
  • Parking: Not needed: every Lisbon trip includes the transport.

Read the boat line. The most-reviewed trip includes a 30-minute boat to Benagil “subject to weather conditions and availability”. One other trip includes it too. On the rest the boat is an optional extra, or, on “Lisbon: Explore the Algarve Coast & Benagil Cave by Boat”, a fixed stop whose ticket is not included and is not refunded if the sea cancels the boat. The van goes whatever the weather; the boat does not.

Before You Book

The access rules apply here too

Wherever you leave from, the same binding rules govern the cave itself (Edital 019/2024 of the Capitania do Porto de Portimão, in force since 13 August 2024, amended by Edital 009/2025 on 30 July 2025): you cannot land on the beach inside, you cannot swim or float in, and motorised boats may only enter for a brief look. Kayaks and SUPs go in with a guide, one guide for every six craft. It's the same cave under the same law — the town only changes how you get there.

Full detail, with the official sources, is on the access-rules section of the main guide. If any Lisbon operator promises a stop on the beach inside Benagil, that's the pre-2024 experience — check before paying. Swell over about 1.5 m closes the cave regardless of tide: check the live sea conditions before you fix a date.
